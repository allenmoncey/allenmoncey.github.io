Simulation · Statistics

Multi-variant epidemic simulator

SEIR model with testing and vaccination for Delta and Omicron, calibrated to data; co-authored SIRTEM in ACM TSAS.

Role
Model development, calibration, analytics
When
Dec 2020 – Jul 2022, ASU
Advisor
Dr. Giulia Pedrielli

The problem

During COVID-19, decision makers needed to see how testing and vaccination policies would play out when several variants circulated at once. The same skills apply directly to production systems: simulating a stochastic process, calibrating it to data and testing what-if scenarios.

Approach

  1. Build a multi-variant SEIR model with testing and vaccination dynamics as delay differential equations in MATLAB (dde23).
  2. Calibrate the model to observed data with an AR(2) correction.
  3. Clean data and validate the model visually through Tableau pipelines.

Results

≈ 0.28NMSE for Delta and Omicron
ACM TSASpeer-reviewed journal, 2022
M.S. thesismulti-variant spatially informed testing

The calibrated model reached an NMSE of about 0.28 for the Omicron and Delta variants. This work fed into SIRTEM, published in ACM Transactions on Spatial Algorithms and Systems, and my M.S. thesis.

My role

I built the simulator, the calibration and the data pipelines, and co-authored the journal paper.
