Machine learning · Digital twins

AI sensor compression for digital twins

An AI model that shrinks machine sound data so a digital twin can receive it in real time, and lets low-cost sensors stand in for high-end ones.

High-fidelity audio from low-cost sensors · 2026 paper

Generative models · Audio

Complex-valued autoencoder for machinery audio

A neural network that learns to rebuild CNC machine sound, so virtual factories and VR training can sound like the real machine.

Near-exact sound reconstruction · 2025 paper

Metal additive manufacturing · Robotics

Robotic wire-laser metal DED cell

Metal 3D printing research on an industrial robot cell: a Meltio wire-laser head on a FANUC R-1000iA/80F robot with a 2-axis positioner.

Software–robot faults fixed; cell back in service

Computer vision · Quality

Vision inspection of bandsaw teeth with M.K. Morse

Camera-based inspection that measures each saw tooth’s front edge and flags defective teeth on a statistical control chart. Built with M.K. Morse; presented at NAMRC 53.

Good vs defective teeth separated · NAMRC 53
