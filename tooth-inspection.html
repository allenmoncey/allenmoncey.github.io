Bandsaw tooth inspection with M.K. Morse

Automated vision inspection that measures tooth wear from profile contours, built with industry partner M.K. Morse Co.

Role
Pipeline design and development
Partner
M.K. Morse Co.
When
ASU research, 2023 –

The problem

Bandsaw blade quality depends on tooth geometry, and checking it by eye is slow, subjective and hard to scale across production. M.K. Morse wanted an objective, repeatable measure of tooth condition.

Approach

  1. Image the blade teeth.
  2. Extract each tooth’s profile contour from the image.
  3. Compare profiles to compute a quantitative wear metric.
  4. Flag defective or worn teeth automatically.

Results

Objectivequantitative wear metric replaces visual judgment
Scalableautomated pipeline instead of manual inspection

The system replaced manual inspection with an automated, scalable vision pipeline built around a quantitative wear metric.

My role

I designed and built the defect-detection pipeline, from contour extraction to the wear metric, working with the industry partner.
