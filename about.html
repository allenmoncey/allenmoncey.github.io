About

From the production floor to manufacturing AI.

I started as a mechanical engineer running production schedules and purchase orders. That taught me what a factory actually needs from data: fewer surprises, shorter cycles, on-time delivery. My industrial engineering degree added statistics and experiment design, and my PhD at Arizona State University is about making AI work under real shop-floor limits: bandwidth, edge hardware and noisy sensors.
