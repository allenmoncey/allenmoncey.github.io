Machining digital twin

Bring machining-center CAD into an interactive simulation environment.

Work completed

Created machining-center CAD models in Fusion 360 and integrated them into Unreal Engine 5.1 for digital twin experiments and VR tool-failure simulations.

My contribution

CAD modeling, engine integration, and tool-failure simulation.

Tools & methods: Fusion 360 · Unreal Engine · VR

Public demo to build

Create a small machining-cell scene with a simulated tool-failure event and recorded or synthetic sensor playback.

  1. Use a simplified CAD asset you own and document the import workflow.
  2. Implement normal and tool-failure states with visible state transitions.
  3. Add timestamped sensor playback and label simulated inputs.
  4. Record a 60–90 second walkthrough and provide setup instructions.

Evidence to show

Scene screenshots, a walkthrough video, a state diagram, and documented playback behavior.

Current evidence boundary

Do not describe synthetic playback as live machine integration or a validated predictive-maintenance system.
