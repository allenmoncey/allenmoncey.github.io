Digital twins · VR

Machining-center digital twin in Unreal Engine 5

CAD models of machining centers brought into Unreal Engine 5.1 for real-time digital-twin experiments and VR tool-failure scenarios.

Role
CAD modeling, engine integration, simulation
When
Nov 2022 – Aug 2023, ASU
Advisor
Dr. Ashif Iquebal

The problem

Tool failures on machining centers are costly and unsafe to reproduce on purpose. A virtual replica lets engineers and operators study those scenarios without risking a real machine.

Approach

  1. Model machining centers in Autodesk Fusion 360.
  2. Import and integrate the models into Unreal Engine 5.1 for real-time experiments.
  3. Build VR scenarios of tool failure.
  4. Use the scenarios to analyze failures and inform prevention strategies for the physical machines.

Results

UE 5.1real-time machining scene
VRtool-failure scenarios

The VR scenarios were used to simulate and analyze tool failures and to inform failure-prevention strategies for the physical machining centers.

My role

I built the CAD models, integrated them into Unreal Engine and designed the tool-failure simulations.
